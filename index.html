Why so few people own livestock

Owning sheep is the easy part. Raising them every single day is not.

You need land. Feed. Housing. Daily care. And people who know what they are doing, every single day, rain or shine.

That daily work is what keeps most people out. OwnerSheep removes it. In Aliyah Rizq's words, individuals can take part in sheep ownership "without having to manage a farm themselves."

So who does the farming? A family that started with 30 animals and never stopped.

The story behind it

From 30 goats and sheep in Mersing to 300 acres near Desaru.

Aliyah Rizq Group began as a family-run farm with 30 goats and sheep, serving aqiqah and korban needs for the Muslim community. Founder Madam Siti Alawiyah started it as Aliyah Rizq Farm on a small 2-acre farm in Mersing.

From those roots in Mersing, the group expanded to Desaru and beyond. Today Aliyah Rizq Farm sits on 300 acres of farmland near Bandar Penawar, Johor, and is home to over 1,000 livestock, with sheep breeds such as Dorper, Suffolk and Santa Ines.

Founder and CEO DS Ashraf Bakar has been farming livestock for over 10 years. In 2019 his business was awarded the Malaysian Book of Records for "The Largest Number of Sheep Slaughtered for Korban", in Johor.

OwnerSheep puts that experience to work for you. Participants are assigned sheep under the programme, and Aliyah Rizq manages daily operations including feeding, care and housing.

Straight answers

What's the minimum?

The minimum is 4 sheep to start. The team will explain pricing for the 4-sheep minimum when they get in touch.

How does the end of the programme work?

Aliyah Rizq's OwnerSheep enquiry form states: "Each sheep is bred over 3 years and can produce up to 15 offspring." and "At the end of the program, your flock is sold, and you receive your share of the profits." Ask the team for the full terms, including how your share is worked out.

Can I use my sheep for Qurban?

On aliyahrizq.com: "Owners may choose to utilise the livestock for purposes such as Qurban or other permitted uses." Ask the team how this works alongside the end-of-programme sale for your sheep.

What if a sheep falls ill or dies?

Aliyah Rizq manages daily care. Ask the team about care and replacement terms for OwnerSheep before you decide.

Do I need to know anything about farming?

No. You can own a sheep while Aliyah Rizq manages the daily care, feeding and farm operations.
